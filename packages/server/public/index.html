Grill Me

Your coding agent has questions. Grill Me turns them into swipe cards on your phone. Your answers go straight back into the agent's session, and grill-me.ai can't read any of it.

It's a free, open-source taster for Agent Hub: the same grill UI, without accounts.

How it works

  1. Your agent prints one short link (and a QR code) and waits.
  2. You open it on your phone and answer by swiping.
  3. The answers land in the agent's session within a second, end-to-end encrypted.
